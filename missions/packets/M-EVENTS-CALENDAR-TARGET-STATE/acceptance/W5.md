- **What the three rounds changed, and why it matters:**
  1. **Editing in place replaced returning to draft.** D49 taken literally would
     have shown an event with 37 invitations on the _public_ calendar as a
     draft for the length of an edit, and indefinitely if the operator were
     interrupted — because D4 makes drafts public. D49 is superseded for the
     amendment path.
  2. **Silence became a deliberate act.** Brian asked whether any path left
     people un-notified of a venue change. It did, and the mockup could not
     show him where, because the notify state was a read-only chip with no
     control behind it. `W5-03b` is the screen that was missing.
  3. **One notify decision, not one per field.** A person receives one message
     saying the event changed, never one per changed field.
- **Carried into the packet:**

    of an amendment. The last is **another mission's brief** and is flagged as

  - **Saving an amendment holds the event's unsent messages** until the change
    is assessed. This mission places the hold; Mission 4 decides what resumes.
