## Acceptance contract

1. The existing table, filters, sorting, question columns and three headline
   numbers behave exactly as they do today. No second table, no new page.
2. Delivery reports the real state of each person's most recent job in the
   vocabulary already shipped: Queued, Attempted, Delivered, Failed, Retryable.
   **Nothing queued** remains where it is true.
3. The Delivery filter selects on those states, and **Needs attention** returns
   exactly the failed and retryable people.
4. A person who has not answered shows the rung already sent and the next one
   due. An answered person shows no chase position.
5. An escalated person reads **Escalated to the President** and shows no further
   player-facing rung.
6. A person whose answer arrives reads **Chase stopped** in the same transaction
   that cancels their remaining jobs.
7. Somebody with no usable route reads **Not dispatched — no channel** and is
   counted rather than silently absent.
8. Chase position and Delivery are operator-tier. A club-link holder sees
   neither, proved by test.
9. No provider identifier, template id, phone number or message body appears on
   this surface.
10. Reading this surface writes nothing and dispatches nothing.

## Boundary confirmed at approval

Seeing that eight people have not answered is not chasing them. W4 never
chases (W5), never repairs (W6), and never changes the audience (W1). The chase
position is a reading of `notification_jobs`, not a new record: W7 owns the
offsets that decide the times shown, W5 owns escalation, W6 owns failure.
