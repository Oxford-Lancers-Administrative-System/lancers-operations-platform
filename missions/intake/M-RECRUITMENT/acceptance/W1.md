## How these screens were made

Both sides are photographs of the same running application at
`main@e669331`, on the `mission-m-recruitment-1` lease, at a browser-measured
1280px and a browser-measured 375px. The proposed side is the same page with the
proposal evaluated into its DOM, so the two sides differ only by the change.

Every element in the proposal is cloned from the shipped roster board rather
than authored. That is how Brian's 2026-08-31 instruction — _"similar
structures... and similar colors, if we can keep them consistent"_ — is enforced:
mechanically, not by eye.

## What is synthetic, and what is real
