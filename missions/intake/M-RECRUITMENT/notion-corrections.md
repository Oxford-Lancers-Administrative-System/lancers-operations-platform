## Why these two rows exist

Both recorded wordings describe a rule Brian has now replaced. His 2026-08-31
ruling — _"The rule for recruits is never 'chased.' It's getting the wrong
signal… it should never be harsh. That's the better rule… We should send polite
reminders, nudges, things like that."_ — supersedes both the 2026-08-25 "exactly
one message, ever" gloss and the 2026-08-28 "never chased" clarification that
replaced it. Recruitment is sales prospecting: the prohibition is on obligation
and escalation, not on contact. Left uncorrected, the two records would tell a
future reader that the platform must not send a recruit a second message, which
is the opposite of what the mission is now being built to do.

**Both proposals were redrafted on 2026-08-31.** The earlier drafts argued the
never-chased rule and were never applied, so nothing in Notion needs unwinding.
