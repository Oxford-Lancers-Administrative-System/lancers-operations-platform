2. Narration instead of screens

The first full build appended cards of label/value rows describing what a screen would do, next to an untouched page. Brian: "it didn't actually make the fucking screens. It just showed what it wanted to do." These were swept, but the habit reappeared as thin work elsewhere — see W10 below.

The correct technique, and it does work: insert the proposal into the real DOM. fill() real fields, afterField() to place a block between real fields, rebuildCard()/recordRow() to replace a real card's rows with cloned markup. W5 and W2 are the two examples worth copying.
