W1 · Look up any person the club holds

An operator finds any human connected to the club and reads what is known about them. Today that is impossible: no person route exists anywhere in the application, and a Person can only be reached through a membership or an operator seat. Both surfaces this workflow introduces — /operate/people and /operate/people/[personId] — are new, and nothing occupies either path on main.

Reading this review. Every screen shows two sides, always labelled the same way and always banded the same way: Current — on main today and Proposed — this mission. Each screen head also states whether the surface is New or Modified.

Every screen here is New, so every current side reads “New surface, nothing to compare”. That is the grounding rule, not an omission: a surface that does not exist is drawn on both sides, and a surface that exists is photographed on both sides. Never one of each. W5 and W6 redesign the roster, which does exist, so those screens will be photographs.

What binds, and what does not. The mockup owns structure and copy — which sections exist and in what order, what each row carries, which controls exist and where they sit, which states get their own screen. The application owns style: button variants, colour, typography, spacing and shared formatters come from src/theme.ts and MUI defaults. An ugly control here means “a control goes here”.

The one open decision on this workflow is the list columns, which you approved in principle and asked to judge rendered.
