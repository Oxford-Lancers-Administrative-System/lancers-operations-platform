## What this approval settles

**The page carries the mission's decisions rather than diverging from them.**
Person · Onboarding · Season banding, the same three groups and colours as the
board; season facts editing in place with the board's interaction; person facts
rendering and routing to the person record; history in `W8`'s shape.

**The per-item `Resolve … ▾` / `SAVE` pair is retired.** An onboarding item is
the same edit as every other value and now looks like one. Mission 7 still owns
what the items mean and when they block activation.

**The separate `Membership status` card is folded into the Standing field**,
which takes the shipped activation control with it. Drawn rather than assumed
because it touches shipped behaviour, and approved as drawn.

**A departed membership renders complete and read-only**, with the activation
control absent rather than disabled.

**A durable person fact is editable in exactly one place in this mission** — the
person record, under `W2`'s rules. The board, player detail and the People list
all show those facts and all route to the same place to change them. Three edit
paths writing one field would mean `W2`'s reason-and-supersede rule either
followed them everywhere or quietly did not apply.

## Carried, not settled here

**The status history reads `Onboarding → Onboarding` on two rows**, because the
enum migration maps `carried_forward` and `confirmed` onto the same rung. It is a
migration question rather than a surface one, and it is still open: migrate them
as no-ops, collapse them, or keep the old labels on historical rows.
