## What this approval settles

**The comparison is field by field**, not "the survivor wins". The older record
is often the more complete and the newer the more correct, and assuming either
loses data. Fields that agree are still shown, so the operator can see the
comparison was complete.

**Two memberships in one season refuse the merge.** Merging them silently would
rewrite a season's squad; the roster is where that is resolved.

**There is no undo.** The losing row surviving, marked merged and pointing at the
survivor, is what makes a manual repair possible; a real undo would be a second
destructive path. The confirmation states what will move before it moves.

**Nothing is ever deleted** — invariant I6 — and a merge reads as one audited
event, not a scatter of field edits.
