## What this approval settles

**The queue names which facts are missing, per row**, rather than a count. A
count sends the operator to the record to find out what the queue already knows.

**It shows an absence and never a value.** A row says _emergency contact_; it
never shows one. That keeps the most disclosing fields in this mission off its
most routine screen.

**Filter by which fact is missing**, because that is how the work batches — one
afternoon of emergency contacts, not one person at a time.

**Both entry points**, settled earlier as amendment `W1-A1` and applied: the
linked count on the roster and the People list, and the **Missing data** entry in
Administration.

**A row that cannot be satisfied persists.** There is no per-fact `refused` or
`not applicable` state — Task 11 §1 puts that in Mission 7, and inventing one
here would pre-empt it. `W7-07` draws the consequence: a departed alumnus with no
personal email sits in the queue indefinitely. The gap is accepted, not hidden.

**Status**, not Standing. Renamed across every surface 2026-08-27 after Brian
asked what the word meant; it had never been an approved term and collided with
_alumni standing_ on the same record.
