## What this approval settles

**The roster is a board.** Eighteen columns in three labelled, tinted groups —
Person, Onboarding, Season — wide, scrolling sideways, with the Player column
pinned. `W5-05` is the same page scrolled to its right-hand end.

**It writes.** This workflow was specified read-only and is not any more. Season
facts edit in the cell: one click opens it, the change commits on its own, a
dropdown appears only where the value set is fixed, and every commit writes an
audit event onto the person's history without asking for a reason. Person facts
render on the board and open the person record instead, because changing one is
an override and `W2` owns what that costs.

**This mission builds the columns it shows.** Positions, jersey, coach group,
formalwear, Blues, eligibility and availability have no storage on `main`. Taken
deliberately as scope; Mission 9 still owns what the football fields mean.

**Filtering is three things.** A pinned set of Status, Availability and Missing
onboarding data; a caret in every other column header; and a `Filtered by` chip
bar carrying every active filter with `Clear all`. A pinned control and its
column header are **one filter with two controls**, each reflecting the other.

The chip bar is load-bearing rather than decorative: `W5-01` has Coach group set
on a column scrolled far off to the right, and without the bar the board would
look mysteriously short with nothing to say why.

**Two limits, both recorded rather than assumed.** Date of birth and emergency
contact are not on this board and cannot be — Task 08 §6 keeps them off every
list. Raw email and phone are gone too, replaced by indicators.
