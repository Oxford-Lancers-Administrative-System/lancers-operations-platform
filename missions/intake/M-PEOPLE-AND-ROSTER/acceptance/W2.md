## What this approval settles, after three rounds

**It is one page.** Every screen but two renders the whole edit surface, and each
head names its route. The two exceptions say so: `W2-06` is the person record
after the save, `W2-08` is `W4`'s comparison.

**Validation is a requirement, not an assumption.** An email must be an email; a
phone must be a plausible number **from any country**, because the club's people
come from all over the world. A bare national number is the only case where the
club's location decides anything. The negative cases carry equal weight and are
acceptance criteria in their own right: refusing a correct number is the worse
failure, since it stops an operator recording something true.

**The WhatsApp consequence is on the screen.** Where the number being replaced is
on WhatsApp for the active season, `W2-05` says so before the save, and says what
follows. It never claims the new number is or is not on WhatsApp.

**The merge is a handoff, not a button into the dark.** `Compare with …` and
`W2-08` showing where it lands.
