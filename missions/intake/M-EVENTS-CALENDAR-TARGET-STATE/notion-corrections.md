## Notes

- The D35 retirement is a **supersession of an approved decision in an
  owner-approved brief**. It is recorded here rather than applied, because no
  agent edits Notion, and because a reader of the brief who never sees this
  packet would otherwise still believe mass delete is required.
- The Capability Register row is deliberately incomplete: the register's exact
  wording has not been fetched, and a correction cannot be proposed against text
  nobody has read.
- **The import-format row is the largest single change.** The 2026-08-18
  amendment committed the club's own term-card spreadsheet as the import
  baseline and made Stewart's confirmation a prerequisite. Brian replaced both
  on 2026-08-21. A reader of the brief who never sees this packet would
  otherwise still be waiting on Stewart for a format nobody is going to build.
- **The Capability Register is the most out-of-date record**, and none of its
  drift originates here — C2, C3, C4, C5b, C6 and C7 were all overtaken by the
  Events brief's own decisions in August, and the brief's F14 already directs
  two of these closures. This packet did not create the gap; it is the first
  thing to read the register against the brief line by line.
- **The D41 row prevents data loss, not a preference.** Applied to the whole
  record rather than field by field, D41 would overwrite a description an
  operator wrote by hand on a draft the moment somebody corrected the template.
  That is the same class of failure as an amendment discarding people's answers.
- **The Task 02 row is a cross-mission consequence, not an Events decision.**
  D5 names a transition — return to draft — that `W5` no longer performs. The
  rule it states still has to hold, so the trigger has to move to the saving of
  an amendment. Mission 4 owns that brief and must be told; this packet may not
  quietly reinterpret another mission's decision.
