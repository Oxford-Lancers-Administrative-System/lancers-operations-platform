## What the draft lost before it was approved

Three corrections on 2026-09-02, all of them the same mistake — inventing where
the record already had something to reuse:

1. **Narrative UI.** Row notes that editorialised ("nothing here blocks
   anything, ever") were the numbered notes' job. The notes now carry only what
   the shipped provenance slot carries: who, and when.
2. **Invented elements.** Item status renders as the record renders it — plain
   underlined `body2`, state name changed, nothing else. Resolution stopped
   being four drawn chips and became the row's own `Select`, clicked open, with
   one option added.
3. **The log was counts.** "That is just not useful… I want to see the
   individual items that come underneath, when it was asked versus when it was
   received." It is now the record's own `StatusHistory` markup, one entry per
   ask and per answer.

Two shoots were also refused outright by `must()` before any of that — once for
a heading the helpers had guessed at by measuring font size, once for a status
pill that contradicted the shipped status beside it. Both refusals were correct.
