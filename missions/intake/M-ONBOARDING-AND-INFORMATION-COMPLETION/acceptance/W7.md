## The one locked at its recommendation

`T07-merge-precedence`. `season_messaging_consents` is unique on
`(person_id, season_id)`, so merging two people who both hold a consent row for
the same season must choose one — the database refuses rather than picks. The
specification recommended that **the most restrictive state wins, not the most
recent**: if either record says `refused` or `withdrawn`, the survivor is
`refused` or `withdrawn`, because consent is permission to contact somebody and
a record-keeping operation must never manufacture permission a person declined.

Brian approved the specification as written without separating that row, so it
is recorded as locked at the recommendation, the convention `W3`, `W4` and `W5`
used. It has no surface of its own: its only appearance is one more line in
Mission 5's existing merge comparison.
