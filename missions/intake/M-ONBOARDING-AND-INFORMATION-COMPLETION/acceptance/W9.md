## The one locked at its recommendation

What becomes of an escalation when nobody holds the configured office. The
specification recommended **retaining it and showing it in the operator area**,
rather than falling back to a named person or letting it vanish: a message that
silently disappears is worse than one nobody reads, and this is the one message
that exists to catch what everything else missed. Brian approved without
separating that row, so it is locked at the recommendation.
