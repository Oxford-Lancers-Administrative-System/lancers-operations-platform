## What the approval did and did not settle

The approval covers the specification as written, and the specification carried
four decisions at **proposed for owner approval**. Brian approved without
separating them, so each is recorded as **locked at its recommendation** — the
same convention `W3` used. Three of them are safely settled that way:

- **The route.** `/me/[token]/details`, on the credential the player already
  holds. Engineering detail with no product consequence.
- **Where the document text is administered.** `W11`, which already owns
  per-season configuration. It does not block anything here.
- **`relationship`.** Stays on the form as its one optional field. Brian has now
  listed the emergency contact's fields twice without it, so this is the one to
  put back to him cheaply if it ever matters.

**The fourth genuinely leaves the mission.** Whether the photo release needs a
real signature is a club-liability question, not an engineering one, and Brian
took it to the team on 2026-09-02. It is filed as **LAN-213** together with the
five pieces of wording the club owes. The mission builds the dated-agreement
mechanism — version, moment, person — unless the team says otherwise;
e-signature would be additive rather than a rework.
