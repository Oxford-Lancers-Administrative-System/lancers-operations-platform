## What "that" was

The approval is conditional on the ownership statement it followed, which is now
in the specification as **What the flip starts, and who owns each part**.
Brian, 2026-09-01: _"As soon as they get flipped to onboarding, if they have any
outstanding personal items, they should get re-notified... we're going to have a
chase associated with that and everything. I don't think that gets built here."_

`onboarding-opened` is one trigger that is both the welcome and the start of the
chase clock. W3 fires it inside the flip's transaction and owns nothing
downstream. The mandatory personal fields are chased as **one derived checklist
item**, not a separate errand. A flipped recruit is chased identically to any
other arrival.

## The one worth revisiting

`membership_entry` stays `('new','returning')`. That was recorded because the
approval covered the specification as written, and the specification recommended
it — **but Brian did not address it in his own words, and the photograph is an
argument against it.** On `W3-01` the Entry field reads `New` and nothing on the
record names recruitment, so a flipped recruit is indistinguishable from a
hand-added player on the surface an operator actually works from.

It is recorded as locked so the ledger is not ambiguous, and flagged here and in
`HANDOFF.md` as the first thing to put back to Brian if it bites. Changing it is
a schema and vocabulary change and would need his decision.
