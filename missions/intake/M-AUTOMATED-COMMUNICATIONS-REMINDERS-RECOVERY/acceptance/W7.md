## Acceptance contract

1. `/operate/admin/messaging`, titled **Messaging schedule**, under
   Administration beside Operators and Roles. Not "Delivery" — that word already
   means the per-event telemetry W6 owns.
2. Editable **per event type, never per event**. ADR 0021's no-per-event-override
   rule survives intact.
3. Each type carries: player RSVP by, first invitation sent, reminder cadence,
   how many reminders of each kind, and president escalation.
4. Defaults: cadence **24 hours**, **2 WhatsApp and 1 email**, escalation **12
   hours after the RSVP deadline** for every type.
5. The ladder order is fixed and stated, not configurable. Only spacing and
   counts are tuned.
6. Everything is counted from the **event start**, not a fixed clock.
7. **There are no quiet hours**, stated on the page.
8. **Reminders count forward from the invitation** on the cadence.
9. Every row expands to preview the dates it produces, for an event four weeks
   out approved today, and warns when the last reminder lands well before the
   deadline.
10. On a phone the page renders one card per event type, matching LAN-157's
    participation card idiom. No horizontal scrolling.
11. Changes apply only to events approved afterwards; approved events keep the
    schedule they were approved with. Every change is attributed.
12. **Late approval:** the invitation goes immediately, still states the RSVP
    deadline, fills the remaining time with WhatsApp reminders on the normal
    cadence, always sends at least one WhatsApp, is **WhatsApp only**, and
    **never escalates to the President**. The event still appears in W5's queue.

## Open at approval

Brian approved the workflow with two values unsettled, as
`proposed for owner approval` on its decision table:

- **The first-invitation values.** Counting forward makes them derivable:
  `deadline + (reminders × cadence)` gives 5 days for a practice, 10 for a game
  and 8 for a social. The earlier 7/21/14 were built for a different model and
  leave a game's ladder finishing eleven days before its own deadline.
- **Whether that column stays editable or becomes derived.** Recommended:
  editable, with the row's preview warning when the last reminder lands well
  before the deadline — announcing a game early is legitimate, and a warning
  informs rather than refuses.
