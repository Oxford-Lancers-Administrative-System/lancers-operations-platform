## Acceptance contract

1. **The surface is the application's own.** The hold panel is built from the
   components the operator shell already uses — the same alert and radio group
   the edit page uses today — not a second visual language. The mockup's inline
   styling approximates them and is not the specification; the components are.
2. Saving an amendment holds every unsent job for that event, and holds nothing
   else.
3. The panel names the held messages and when each was due, before the operator
   commits.
4. It states that nothing already sent is retracted.
5. Choosing to tell people resumes the held messages **and** informs everybody
   already invited of what changed. Declining resumes them unchanged.
6. No job remains held once the amendment resolves or the event is cancelled.
   Held is never a resting state.
7. A reschedule recomputes the response deadline and every derived time using
   W7's rules, and the application says a reschedule is happening.
8. A reschedule leaving no runway follows W7's late-approval rule: immediate,
   WhatsApp only, at least one message, no escalation.
9. Cancelling an event cancels every undelivered job, and **none of them appears
   as a delivery failure** in W6's counts, lists or queues.
10. Cancellation notices are sent only where Mission 2 recorded that choice.
11. The internal cancellation reason appears in no recipient-facing payload,
    proved by test against the rendered message.
12. An already-delivered message is never retracted and history is never
    rewritten.
13. A player's standing answer survives an amendment, a reschedule and a
    cancellation.
