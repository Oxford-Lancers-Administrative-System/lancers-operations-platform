## Acceptance contract

1. The first WhatsApp message is a plain invitation: event, when, where,
   deadline, and the two actions. It carries no count of any kind.
2. Social proof begins at the second rung and counts **attendance only**. No
   player-facing message reports how many people have not answered.
3. The WhatsApp tap is the answer. Neither landing page asks the player to
   confirm an answer they have already given.
4. A No is standing from the click with the visible default **No reason given**.
   No copy implies the No is unrecorded until a reason arrives.
5. On the No page **Change to Yes** is primary and **Give a reason and continue**
   is the single forward action.
6. A Yes stands while required event questions remain outstanding. An incomplete
   Yes earns exactly one further message, never a second chase ladder.
7. Every answer ends on the player's own page: work needing an answer above,
   everything already answered whose event has not happened yet below, both Yes
   and No, each changeable.
8. An empty queue reads **No outstanding events**, keeps the answered list
   reachable, and offers the public calendar.
9. An approved event is visible to its invitee before its invitation is
   dispatched. Answering early suppresses the rung rather than sending it.
10. Emphasis points at Yes on every surface. Every **Change to No** is available
    and unfilled, including where it is the only control in its row.
11. Answer colour matches `src/app/participation/participation-table.tsx`: Yes
    `success`, No `error`, anything else `default`.
12. The page is reached by a person-scoped credential associated with a season,
    which stops resolving when that season closes and is revocable per person
    without waiting for Mission 10.
