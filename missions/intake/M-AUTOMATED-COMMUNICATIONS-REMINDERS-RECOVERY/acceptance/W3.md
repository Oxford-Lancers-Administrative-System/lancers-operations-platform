## Acceptance contract

1. **Record answer** appears only against an invitation with no answer at all,
   on the desktop row and in the 375 card.
2. Recording writes an ordinary response with `source = operator` and the
   recording operator's person id.
3. **The row then just says Yes.** No badge, no "recorded by", no second line.
   It counts in the headline numbers like any other answer.
4. Who recorded it, and when the player said it, live in the audit trail.
   `responded_at` stays distinct from `recorded_at`.
5. A No requires a real reason, in the form and at the database.
6. `responded_at` may be backdated, never postdated, and never before the
   invitation.
7. The event's own questions are answerable in the same form. Partial answers
   are accepted and never block the answer; unanswered ones stay outstanding.
8. Recording cancels that person's pending reminders and clears an un-actioned
   nonresponse flag in the same transaction, and they leave W5's chase list.
9. Superseding an answer the player gave themselves is out of scope. A player
   changing their mind does it on their own page.
10. A recorded RSVP never implies attendance, which remains Task 04's.
