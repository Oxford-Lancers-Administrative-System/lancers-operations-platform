## Acceptance contract

1. LAN-78's delivery page is the surface repair happens on. Its counts report
   real numbers, and Audience continues to report the frozen audience.
2. **Retries are automatic and offer no operator action.** The row shows which
   attempt and when the next is due.
3. **The email fallback is automatic.** No operator sends it, routes to it, or
   confirms it.
4. A WhatsApp failure that email then carried reads **WhatsApp unresponsive**,
   stays counted, and offers no action. The person was reached; the club's
   primary channel failed and that must be visible rather than silently
   substituted.
5. **Not dispatched — no channel** is the only state requiring a person, and what
   it requires is a roster fix. It is counted, visible, and links to the record.
6. Repair never becomes a place where somebody composes a message.
7. Diagnostics is a **page**, reached from the delivery page, using the
   application's own table and chrome. It is not a dialog.
8. Diagnostics shows one row per **attempt** per channel — person, channel,
   attempt number, when, outcome, provider reference — including the fallback
   email that carried a failed WhatsApp.
9. Webhooks are deduplicated; a provider reporting the same result twice appears
   once.
10. No message content appears on either surface.
11. A cancelled job never appears as a failure — W8 owns that distinction.
12. A delivery failure never alters an RSVP, and an RSVP never clears a delivery
    failure.
